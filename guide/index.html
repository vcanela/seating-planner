Seating Planner

· User Guide Open App

The planner does two jobs that share one student list: build a seating chart for your room, and split the class into random groups. Everything lives behind the gear icon (top-right) and the Seating / Groups switch at the top. Here's the whole thing, click by click.

🗂️

Getting set up

1

🏫 Create or select a class

Click the gear icon in the top-right corner to open the setup panel. The class name sits at the very top of the panel — click it to rename. Just below, use the dropdown to switch between classes, or the New, Copy and delete buttons beside it.

Each class remembers its own room layout, students, rules, groups, and last results — independently.

2

🪑 Draw the room layout

On the Room tab, set the number of rows and columns to match your classroom. Then click Click to Edit Seat Layout — the panel stays open and you click or drag directly on the grid to switch individual seats on or off. Leave gaps where there are no desks.

Click Done Editing Layout when you're finished.

Tip: The top of the grid is always the front of the room (the Board / Front of Class bar), so students can orient themselves.
3

👩‍🎓 Add your students

Switch to the Students tab. Type names separated by commas or new lines, then press Enter or click Add to List. Duplicate names are skipped automatically. Remove a name by hovering it and clicking the ✕.

This one student list is shared by both the seating chart and the groups.

🪑

Make a seating chart

4

⚙️ Set seating rules optional

On the Rules tab, pick a rule type, choose the student(s), and click Add Rule:

⬆️ Sit at Front Keeps a student in the front rows. ↔️ Keep Separated Keeps two students apart from each other. 📍 Fixed Seat Pins a student to one exact seat every time — ideal for a wheelchair user or a fixed accessibility need. Pick the student, then choose the seat (listed as Row / Seat).

The Flexibility bar shows how constrained the chart is overall — lots of rules mean less variation between reshuffles.

Use Hide to collapse the rules list if students are nearby and you'd rather they didn't read it.

5

🔀 Generate & refine

Make sure Seating is selected in the top-bar switch, close the panel, and click Generate. The planner tries 200 random arrangements and keeps the one that best satisfies your rules.

Click Reshuffle any time for a fresh arrangement — the banner tells you what percentage of seats changed. Need a tweak? Drag a student to another seat to swap the two.

The date the chart was set shows under the grid, so you can tell at a glance whether you've already refreshed it this week.

Print: Click Print for a clean landscape page — class name, date, the board marker, and no on-screen clutter.
👥

Form random groups

6

🔁 Switch to Groups mode

At the top of the screen, click Groups in the Seating / Groups switch. The main area changes to group cards, and the setup panel now shows just two tabs: Students (the same shared list) and Groups.

Your seating chart isn't lost — flip back to Seating any time and it's exactly as you left it.

7

🔢 Choose group size & rules

Open the Groups tab. Under Grouping, pick how you want to split the class:

Number of groups You set how many groups; the planner spreads students evenly across them. Students per group You set the size; the planner works out how many groups that makes.

Use the big − and + buttons to adjust the number, or click the box and type it directly.

Then add group rules the same way as seating — pick the type, choose two students, and click Add Rule:

🤝 Same Group Two students always end up together (this chains — link A&B and B&C and all three stay together). 🚫 Keep Apart Two students are always placed in different groups.
8

🎲 Form groups & refine

Click Form Groups in the top bar. The planner tries 300 arrangements and keeps the one that balances group sizes while respecting every rule. Click Reshuffle Groups for a different split.

The result shows as clear, colour-coded cards — one per group, easy for students to read off the board. Want to move someone? Drag their name onto another group's card.

Print: The Print button gives a landscape page of the group cards with the class name and date.
💾

Saving & sharing

💾

Import & Export

At the bottom of the setup panel there's a strip with Export class, All classes, and Import. Save a single class or your whole library as a file, hand it to a colleague, or move it to another device by importing it back. Imported classes are added alongside your existing ones — nothing is overwritten.

Everything is stored in your browser on this device; nothing is sent to a server. So it's worth exporting a backup now and then.
